# Privacy policy — Time Architect

**Last updated: 2026-10-24**
Contact: <bynrnworld@gmail.com>

## Summary

Time Architect does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. The game runs entirely on your
device and works offline.

## What is stored

The game stores the following on your device only, in browser `localStorage`:

- language preference;
- settings (volumes, visual quality, accessibility options);
- campaign progress (restored chambers, best tick counts, unlock position, dismissed hints).

A rolling backup copy of the save is kept so a corrupted save can be recovered.

## What is not collected

- No account, name, email or phone number.
- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or tracking pixels.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
- No network requests are made by the game at runtime. The only network activity is loading the
  files of the game itself from the host you chose.

## Third parties

The build includes no third-party SDKs. Flag images are local files from Wikimedia Commons
(public domain). System fonts are provided by your operating system. There are no ads and no ad
networks.

## Your control

- Clear site data in your browser to erase all progress and settings.
- Use **Settings → Reset progress** inside the game to erase campaign progress while keeping settings.
- Uninstalling the PWA removes its cached files.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds online services, analytics or advertising, this policy and the in-game
privacy screen will be updated before that version ships.
