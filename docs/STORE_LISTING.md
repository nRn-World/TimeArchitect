# Store listing draft

## Positioning

A calm, precise time-manipulation puzzler. Not a reflex game, not a horror game: a clockwork
campaign about holding one moment still while another keeps moving.

**Age rating:** Everyone / PEGI 3 / Apple 4+ (no violence, no chat, no user data).
**Price:** Free download with the full campaign unlocked in this build.
**Planned monetization:** Full Campaign unlock $3.99, Atelier cosmetic pack $1.99 — see
[MONETIZATION.md](MONETIZATION.md). No billing SDK is wired yet, so the store listing must currently
declare **no in-app purchases**.
**Ads:** None, and none planned for launch.
**Data collection:** None. The app makes no network requests during play.
**Application ID / bundle ID:** `com.nrnworld.timearchitect`
**Privacy policy URL:** to be published by the owner (text ready in `PRIVACY.md`).
**Support URL / email:** `bynrnworld@gmail.com`

## English

**Title:** Time Architect
**Short (80 chars):** Rewind, freeze and fast-forward objects to solve clockwork chambers.
**Long:**
The chamber clock only moves when you do. Every step advances a dozen mechanisms: bridges that
forget, gates that keep their own time, drones that sweep the galleries. You can rewind a dial, hold
it still, or send it racing ahead — but only one moment can be frozen at a time. Read the rhythm,
freeze the span, and cross before the world remembers you.

- 12 handcrafted chambers, from a single bridge to a three-clock finale
- One clean rule: a gate that closes beneath you unmakes you
- Free undo, instant restart, no timers, no ads
- Seven languages, full Arabic RTL, offline play
- Reduced motion, high contrast, larger touch targets, keyboard and gamepad

By nRnWorld.
